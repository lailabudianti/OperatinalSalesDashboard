# 🛒 Blinkit – Sales Performance Dashboard

This project analyzes Blinkit's sales data to understand the total sales distribution and business performance patterns across various outlets. Through an interactive dashboard, the analysis focuses on product contribution and sales segmentation based on fat content, as well as performance differences based on size, location, and outlet type. The insights generated aim to provide a data-driven overview of business performance as a context for decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project aims to analyze the sales performance of Blinkit, an instant grocery platform, by utilizing historical sales data and outlet characteristics. This dashboard presents a summary of key KPIs to provide a comprehensive overview of business performance.

This analysis is important to support data-driven decision making, particularly in understanding sales drivers, product performance, and differences in performance between outlets based on size, location, and outlet type.

---

🔍 Business Questions

The analysis in this project is designed to answer the following questions:

- What is the **overall sales performance** based on key KPIs, with Total Sales as the main indicator?
- What is the **distribution of total sales based on product fat content**, and how are other KPIs presented as supporting context?
- What item types contribute the most to **total sales**?
- How does **the distribution of total sales** differ based on outlet size, outlet location, and outlet type?
- Is there a **pattern in the distribution of total sales** based on the year the outlet was established?

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

This dataset contains retail sales data from a grocery company (Blinkit) that includes product information, outlet characteristics, and sales performance.

The data covers key attributes such as product category, fat content, outlet type and size, outlet location, year of establishment, sales metrics, and customer ratings. This dataset is used to analyze sales distribution patterns and compare performance between products and outlets based on total sales.

The analysis focuses on **Total Sales as the main indicator of business performance**, while other metrics are used as supporting context for interpretation.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Breakdown & Insights

This dashboard provides flexibility to view performance based on various metrics. However, the main insight focuses on **Total Sales** because the differences in other metrics such as **Average Sales, Number of Items, and Average Rating** are relatively limited and serve more as supporting context.

![KPI Overview](https://github.com/lailabudianti/OperatinalSalesDashboard/blob/main/image/Overview%20KPI.jpg)

Displaying a summary of Total Sales, Average Sales, Number of Items, Average Rating 

**Insight:**

- Total sales reached **$1.20M** with an average sales value of **$141 per transaction**.

**Insight:**

### 🔹 Total Sales by Outlet Establishment

![Total Sales by Outlet Establishment](https://github.com/lailabudianti/OperatinalSalesDashboard/blob/main/image/Total%20Sales%20by%20Outlet%20Establishment.jpg)

Line chart of sales trends based on outlet establishment year.

**Insight:**

- Outlets established around **2018 recorded the highest total sales**, indicating that this establishment period correlates with optimal sales performance.
- Outlets established between **2012 and 2017 showed relatively stable total sales**, without extreme fluctuations.
- Newer outlets (after 2019) had lower total sales, **indicating an early growth phase** compared to more mature outlets.

## 📈 Key Insights

Some key insights gained from the analysis:

- Low Fat products contributed more to total sales than Regular products
- Medium-sized outlets located in Tier 3 contributed the most to total sales
- Supermarket Type 1 outlets dominated total sales, with higher item sales volume than other outlet types
- Outlets established around **2018 recorded the highest total sales** compared to other years of establishment
- Several **specific product categories contributed significantly to total revenue**, based on the distribution of total sales per item type

---

## 💡 Business Implications

The analysis results indicate that the company can:

- Sales contribution information based on **fat content and product category** can be used as an **initial reference in product portfolio evaluation**
- Total sales distribution based on **outlet size and location** provides an overview of the areas that currently contribute the most to sales
- Outlets and categories with lower sales contributions can be the **subject of further analysis** to understand potential performance improvements.
- Historical sales patterns based on **the year the outlet was established** can be used as **descriptive insights** in future outlet network analysis.
